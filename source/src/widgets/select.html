Interactive: why randomization matters

Each dot is a worker. One more year of education truly raises wage by 2. Unobserved ability (color, blue to red) also raises wage, and it can also decide who gets more education.

$\gamma=0$ is a randomized experiment. Larger $\gamma$ means ability drives who gets more education (self-selection).
