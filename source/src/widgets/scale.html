Interactive: shifting, scaling and standardizing

$X$ is normal. Build $Y=aX+b$ and compare. Horizontal bars mark $\pm1$ standard deviation around each mean.

$\E[X]$
$\E[aX+b]=a\E[X]+b$
$\var[X]$
$\var[aX+b]=a^2\var[X]$
$\sd[aX+b]=|a|\,\sd[X]$

Changing $b$ slides the curve without changing its width. Changing $a$ stretches it, and the variance changes by $a^2$. A negative $a$ flips it but the variance is the same as for $|a|$. The z-score always has mean 0 and standard deviation 1.
