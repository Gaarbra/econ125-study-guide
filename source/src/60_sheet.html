Moments (Lecture 3)

Expectation

$$\E[X]=\sum x\,f_X(x)$$
$$\E[aX+b]=a\E[X]+b$$

Variance

$$\var[X]=\E[X^2]-\mu^2$$
$$\var[aX+b]=a^2\var[X]$$

z-score

$$Z=\frac{X-\mu}{\sigma}$$

Covariance

$$\cov[X,Y]=\E[XY]-\mu_X\mu_Y$$

Correlation

$$\rho=\frac{\cov[X,Y]}{\sigma_X\sigma_Y},\quad -1\le\rho\le1$$

Variance of a sum

$$\begin{aligned}\var[aX+bY]&=a^2\var[X]+b^2\var[Y]\\&\quad+2ab\cov[X,Y]\end{aligned}$$

Conditioning and estimation (Lecture 4)

Pass-through

$$\begin{gathered}\E[g(X)Y+h(X)\mid X]\\=g(X)\E[Y\mid X]+h(X)\end{gathered}$$

LIE and tower

$$\E\big[\E[Y\mid X]\big]=\E[Y]$$
$$\E\big[\E[Y\mid X,Z]\mid X\big]=\E[Y\mid X]$$

Unbiased and bias

$$\E[W]=\theta,\qquad \Bias=\E[W]-\theta$$

Sample average

$$\E[\bar Y]=\mu$$
$$\var[\bar Y]=\frac{\sigma^2}{n},\quad\se[\bar Y]=\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}$$

Sample variance

$$S^2=\frac{1}{n-1}\sum(Y_i-\bar Y)^2$$
$$\E[S^2]=\sigma^2$$
