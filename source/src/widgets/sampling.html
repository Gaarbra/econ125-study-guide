Interactive: an estimator is a random variable

The population has mean 75 and standard deviation 25 (the slides' setup). Each draw takes a sample of size $n$ and computes the estimator. Repeat many times and the histogram below is its sampling distribution.

Top: the most recent single sample (bars) against the population (dashed). The vertical line is that sample's estimate.

Bottom: the estimates from every sample so far, which is the sampling distribution.
