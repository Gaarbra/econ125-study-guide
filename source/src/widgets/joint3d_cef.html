Interactive 3D: the conditional expectation function is the line through the slices

Slide $x_0$ and watch the dashed conditional density move. Its center, $\E[Y\mid X=x_0]$, traces a straight line on the floor: that line is the CEF, the thing a regression estimates.

Without conditioning you only see the surface. Switch on "Condition on $X$" to see the slices.

Each dashed curve is the distribution of $Y$ among people with $X=x_0$. Its mean sits on the black line, which has slope $\rho\,\sigma_Y/\sigma_X$. With $\rho=0$ the line is flat: knowing $X$ does not move the mean of $Y$, so $\E[Y\mid X]=\E[Y]$. Averaging that line over the distribution of $X$ gives $\E[Y]=0$: the Law of Iterated Expectations.
